The Shepherd & The Jaguar

An Archaeological Thriller of Ancient Secrets and a War Beneath the Earth

An Archaeological Thriller of Ancient Secrets and a War Beneath the Earth

The Mummy meets Indiana Jones with the darker edge of a modern espionage thriller. The Shepherd & The Jaguar is a high-stakes archaeological thriller of ancient bloodlines, forbidden artifacts, hidden enemies, and a secret network built to hold back something buried beneath the Earth.

Three thousand years ago, at La Venta, an ancient ritual meant to bind the Jade Jaguar to the forces protecting the Earth was only partially successful. The shadow split. One part entered the Jaguar. The other disappeared beneath the roots of the ancient ceiba trees. The consequences have endured ever since.

In modern-day Mexico, Marcus is hunted through the streets of Mexico City after the Jade Jaguar awakens and begins warning him of unseen attackers. The pursuit leads him to La Venta, where he encounters Dr. Zuhair and a hidden team fighting to stabilize a failing node of the ancient Lattice. He discovers the Jaguar is part of a network whose nodes are beginning to collapse.

Thousands of kilometres away in Sardinia, Elena is pulled into the same mystery when an obsidian pendant awakens beneath the Giants' Tomb. Her family's ancient duty is finally revealed. The shepherds were never protecting a piece of stone. They were hiding it from something that had been searching for it for thousands of years.

Marcus and Elena are connected across continents through the strange phenomenon known as the Seam. Their artifacts recognize each other, revealing a deeper truth about the Lattice and the bloodline that has guarded it. But the connection also exposes them to the enemy. The Cult has been waiting for this moment. Its agents have infiltrated the ancient nodes, and their true objective is not simply to destroy the guardians. They need the Key.

As the Lattice begins to fail, the guardians are forced out of hiding. Ancient artifacts become weapons. Archaeological sites become battlefields. And the enemy begins closing in from every direction. The guardians can no longer afford to run. Because if the Lattice breaks, the ancient wound beneath the Earth will no longer need to wait for the eclipse. And the thing waiting behind it will be free.

Key Characters

Marcus (Lead Male Protagonist — The Jaguar)

A Mexican tour operator and reluctant guardian of the Jade Jaguar. Carrying the ancient artifact for five years, he is hunted across Mexico and drawn into a conflict he barely understands, discovering the Jaguar is part of an ancient bloodline whose purpose has been misunderstood for three thousand years.

Elena (Lead Female Protagonist — The Shepherd)

A Sardinian shepherdess who has spent her life protecting her family's ancient secret. When the obsidian pendant awakens, Elena is drawn into visions of a forgotten past, becoming capable of guiding and stabilizing the ancient forces within the Lattice.

Matteo (The Key — The Hidden Heir)

Marcus's fifteen-year-old nephew and the unexpected heir to the Jaguar bloodline. Not meant to guard the Gate, but to open it, his emerging ability to control resonance makes him the most important and most vulnerable person in the ancient struggle.

Dr. Lin Xuán (Guardian — The Scholar)

A brilliant archaeologist who investigates the Lattice through evidence, resonance, and reason. As the nodes begin to fail, her scientific understanding is pushed beyond anything conventional archaeology can explain.

Dr. Zuhair (Guardian — The Resonance Expert)

A calm, methodical scholar who carries the Indus Copper Tablet. His understanding of resonance begins to reveal the true nature of the threat: the network is containing a wound in the Earth.

The Enforcer (Primary Antagonist)

A veteran commander of the ancient Cult, utterly committed to his Elders. Disciplined, experienced, and ruthless, he possesses a code that begins to collide with his orders as the Cult's losses mount and his commanders begin to fracture.

Crowe (Cult Strategist)

Cold, analytical, and obsessed with precision, Crowe treats the guardians as a problem to be solved. He directs surveillance, deception, and calculated strikes against the Lattice's nodes, relying on the assumption that everything can be controlled.
