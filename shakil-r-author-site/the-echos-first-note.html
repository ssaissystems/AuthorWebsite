Gentleman Rogue Series · Book 1

The Echo's First Note

A Gentleman Rogue Thriller of Art, Elegance, and the Finest Disguise

A Gentleman Rogue Thriller of Art, Elegance, and the Finest Disguise

The Saint meets Arsène Lupin in a high-stakes caper with the tech of Mission: Impossible and the wit of Jeeves & Wooster. The Echo's First Note is a stylish, emotionally layered adventure thriller about a man who steals nothing for himself—and an AI learning that justice tastes remarkably like good coffee.

The Alfa Romeo Spider purred through Florence's narrow streets, catching the amber light of late afternoon. Kamal Iskandar Ashcroft switched off the ignition. The engine settled with a contented sigh. Then his earpiece chimed. A museum director's Caravaggio had vanished. The insurance company wanted answers. The art world wanted blood. And somewhere beneath the Renaissance stone of his gallery, an artificial intelligence was already running probabilities.

The Caravaggio is not lost. It is being sold—and the man selling it wears a director's title and a philanthropist's smile. What follows is not a heist. It is a restoration—executed with white gloves, absolute silence, and the quiet fury of a man who believes art is memory, not merchandise. Kam recovers the painting, exposes the director, and leaves behind a single handwritten note, perfectly phrased, devastating in its politeness.

In the chaos, Inspector Irene Voss steps into Monte Heritage Recovery with Interpol credentials and a suspicion she cannot shake. She studies Kam with the intensity of someone who has spent seven years building a case file called PROJECT ECHO. She leaves unsatisfied. She will return.

In Monaco, the game escalates. Kam infiltrates a casino floor in a tuxedo, Jeeves feeding probabilities through his earpiece, and dismantles a corrupt banker's operation with surgical precision—until Irene bursts into his suite with a warrant and a certainty. He greets her by name. The fire alarm activates. He is gone before the smoke clears. And in the gallery's basement, Jeeves watches the footage of her face and logs the image longer than the numbers.

Somewhere in Barcelona, Irene pins a photograph to a corkboard. She knows his rhythm. She knows his choices. She knows he returned a painting at three in the morning, wearing white gloves, leaving a note that read: This belonged to everyone. It belongs to everyone again. She will find him. She is certain. But the question will not be whether to arrest him. The question will be whether she still wants to.

Key Characters

Kamal Iskandar / "The Echo" (Lead Protagonist)

Son of a Pakistani diplomat and an Italian noblewoman. Founder of Monte Heritage Recovery, a Florence-based art and insurance consultancy. Elegant, cultured, multilingual, never raises his voice. His moral code is simple: never steal from the honest, never harm children, never become what he fights.

J.E.E.V.E.S. (The AI Companion)

Humanity's first sentient AI, hidden in a server room beneath Kam's gallery. Perfect memory, infinite curiosity, dry humour. Views humans as statistically inefficient, but cannot understand why they willingly fall in love despite overwhelming evidence against it.

Inspector Irene Voss (The Pursuer)

Interpol's Special Investigations Division. Born in Madrid, raised in Barcelona. Her father—a police officer—was killed in the line of duty when she was fourteen. She became the law. Disciplined, relentless, morally rigid. She believes justice is the law, and the law is justice.

The Corrupt Museum Director (Antagonist)

A man who sold masterpieces to private collectors and buried the receipts in offshore accounts. He believes art is an asset class. He believes no one will notice. He is wrong.

The Corrupt Banker (Secondary Antagonist)

Laundering money for art traffickers through a Monaco financial institution. Elegant on the surface, rot beneath. He will learn that elegance is the finest disguise—and that The Echo wears it better.
