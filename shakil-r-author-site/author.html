Meet the Author — Shakil R.

Dr. Shakil R. is a former Pakistan Air Force aerospace engineer, academic, and strategist whose career has spanned aerospace design, defense programs, and higher education. He was a distinguished one-star officer in the Engineering branch of the Pakistan Air Force, retiring at the rank of Air Commodore (equivalent to a Brigadier General).

With a Ph.D. in Computational Fluid Dynamics from Beihang University, China, and a Master's in Flight Dynamics & Control Systems from the University of Texas at Austin, he has served in roles ranging from Engineering Officer in fighter squadrons to Professor and Head of Department, to Commandant of the College of Aeronautical Engineering, and later as Deputy Chief Project Director (Aero) for the JF-17 Project. His work has taken him from the JF-17 aircraft design team in China to leading-edge sustainability and energy projects in Pakistan.

Across four decades, he has combined engineering precision with strategic vision, contributing to aerospace innovation, sustainable energy solutions, and international collaborations. He has published extensively in leading scientific journals, submitted patents in renewable technologies, and founded Smart Green Solutions, a startup dedicated to sustainable energy and education.

Today, he is channeling this unique blend of passion and expertise into fiction. His debut geopolitical thriller, SHADOW: The SENTRY Directive, has been described as "a strategic wake-up call disguised as a thriller," praised for its authenticity, heart, and thought-provoking vision of peace in a fractured world. Drawing on his insider knowledge of aviation, defense, diplomacy, and global strategy, Shakil R. writes with the precision of an engineer and the imagination of a storyteller.

Influenced in his youth by the adventures of Captain W.E. Johns and the deductive brilliance of Sir Arthur Conan Doyle, and later inspired by masters of the modern thriller such as Tom Clancy, Frederick Forsyth, Robert Ludlum, Dan Brown, and Steve Berry, Shakil R. has developed a storytelling style that combines authenticity, suspense, and cinematic pacing.

His work now spans three distinct thriller series:

The Shadow Series

Book 1 Now Available on Amazon

Intelligent geopolitical thrillers exploring diplomacy, espionage, military strategy, and the pursuit of peace in an increasingly unstable world.

The Eclipse Series

Series Premieres Late 2026

Archaeological thrillers where ancient mysteries, forgotten civilizations, and cutting-edge science collide across continents and centuries. Four books planned, with two now in final polish.

The Gentleman Rogue Series

Series Premieres Q3 2027

Sophisticated international adventure thrillers featuring art, history, intrigue, and elegant rogues navigating a world of high society and hidden agendas.

Together, these series reflect his belief that great thrillers should not only entertain but also inspire curiosity, challenge assumptions, and illuminate the complex forces shaping our world.

A life shaped by conflict, innovation, leadership, and global collaboration has prepared him for this new chapter. Whether exploring the fragile balance of international diplomacy, uncovering secrets buried beneath ancient civilizations, or following daring adventures through the world's great cities, Shakil R. invites readers into stories where intelligence, courage, and humanity stand against greed, chaos, and ambition.

ABOUT

Dr. Shakil R., a former military aviation engineer and academic, blends decades of aerospace, defense, and global strategy experience into gripping thrillers. His debut, SHADOW: The SENTRY Directive, delivers authenticity and suspense, launching the Shadow Series. He is currently writing the sequel to The SENTRY Directive, the four-book archaeological thriller series ECLIPSE (premiering late 2026), and the Gentleman Rogue Series of international adventure thrillers (premiering Q3 2027).
