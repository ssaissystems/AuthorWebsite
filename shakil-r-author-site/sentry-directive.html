The SENTRY Directive

A Geopolitical Thriller of Chaos for Profit

A Geopolitical Thriller of Chaos for Profit

Tom Clancy meets John le Carré in an explosive chess match with nuclear stakes. The SENTRY Directive is a tense geopolitical thriller about spies, pilots, and the human cost of invisible wars.

When rising Indian diplomat Arvind Pratap Singh is assassinated in a Kabul bombing, a train is attacked in Pakistan's Balochistan province, and coordinated terror strikes hit Kashmir, the fragile India-Pakistan peace process collapses overnight.

Amid the escalating crisis, two unlikely figures are drawn into the shadows: Wing Commander Sohail Mirza (call sign Shadow), a decorated Pakistan Air Force pilot hardened by personal tragedy, and Ananya Rao, an Indian diplomat scarred by violence.

Evidence points to a covert network orchestrated by Darian Blackwood, a billionaire strategist whose Prometheus Group profits by engineering chaos and instability. Blackwood's mercenary enforcer, codenamed Ahab, executes the dirty work: assassinations, bombings, and disinformation campaigns designed to fracture alliances and keep India and Pakistan locked in perpetual hostility. The goal is not ideology but profit, turning chaos itself into currency.

From Islamabad to Geneva's back rooms, Shadow and Ananya pursue the truth through a labyrinth of betrayals. Shadow, haunted by the loss of loved ones to terror, struggles between loyalty to uniform and the moral clarity of exposing his own side's blind spots. Ananya, driven by duty but burdened by her fiancé's murder in Kabul, wrestles with whether cooperation with an enemy pilot is patriotism or betrayal.

Key Characters

Sohail Mirza (Lead Male Protagonist)

Fighter pilot in Pakistan Air Force (PAF), call sign Shadow. His presence is more felt than seen. Driven by a conflicting sense of justice, vengeance and duty after losing family in the Jaffar Express terrorist bombing executed by Ahab in September 2018.

Ananya Rao (Lead Female Protagonist)

A brilliant and intelligent Indian diplomat, haunted by the death of her love Arvind Pratap Singh, killed as collateral in the Kabul bombing executed by Ahab in March 2015. She is looking for justice and peace among nations.

Ahab (Tactical Antagonist)

A cold, calculating mercenary and tactical genius, orchestrates ruthless attacks on Kabul's embassy, Jaffar Express, Geneva, and Istanbul with surgical precision. Driven by ambition, his enigmatic presence hides a buried past. He evolves into a cunning mastermind, relentlessly pursuing chaos, always eluding capture.

Darian Blackwood (Antagonist — Mastermind)

Born Christopher Mallory. Cold, calculating chaos strategist, delivering every word with surgical precision and emotional detachment. A silken menace; calm, composed, terrifying in restraint. Commanding, predatory, manipulative, strategically cruel. Refined yet ruthless, blending the polish of wealth with the brutality of a hardened past. Head of Prometheus & Ebony Tree.
